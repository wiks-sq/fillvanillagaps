# Fill Vanilla Gaps

**The small things vanilla still doesn't do.** Each feature here was chosen by comparing what players vote for on the official feedback site with the mods they actually keep installed, and keeping the everyday conveniences that fit the vanilla feel.

## Features

- **Inventory & chest sorting.** Press **R** (rebindable) or click the **⇅** button in any chest, barrel, ender chest, shulker box or your inventory. It merges partial stacks and orders items, and never touches your hotbar.
- **Saturation HUD.** A gold outline on the hunger bar shows your hidden saturation. Hold food while hungry to preview what it refills. Food tooltips show hunger and saturation numbers.
- **Right-click harvest.** Right-click a fully grown crop to harvest it, and it replants itself using one seed from the drops. This works on wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart and cocoa.
- **Tree felling.** Crouch and break a log with an axe to fell the whole tree. It only works on natural trees, so log builds are safe.
- **Vein mining.** Crouch and mine an ore with the right pickaxe to break the whole vein (up to 64 blocks).
- **Fast leaf decay.** Leaves cut off from their tree vanish in about half a second and still drop saplings and apples.
- **No more "Too Expensive!"** Anvil jobs cost at most 39 levels.
- **Double doors.** Opening one door opens its pair too. Crouch to open just one.
- **Safe death drops.** Items you drop on death never despawn, survive lava, fire and explosions, and glow through walls.
- **Feather Falling stops crop trampling.**
- **Infinity + Mending** can go on the same bow, as on Bedrock. This is a built-in data pack, so you can turn it off per world.
- **Rotten flesh → leather.** Smoke or smelt it. This is also a toggleable data pack.
- **Terracotta slabs & stairs.** 34 new blocks for plain terracotta and all 16 colors, matching 26.3's concrete and wool sets.

Tree felling and vein mining cost durability and hunger for every extra block, just like normal mining, and stop before your tool breaks.

## Configuration

Every feature can be switched off in `config/fillvanillagaps.json`. The two data packs can be toggled per world with `/datapack`.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **26.3**, Fabric Loader **0.20.1+**, and **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)**
- Install the mod on **both the client and the server**, because it adds blocks.
